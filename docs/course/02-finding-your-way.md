# Finding your way around

*Module 1: The basics · about 10 minutes*

BrainWaves has four columns: the **left rail**, the **sidebar**, the **page** in the middle and
the **right panel**. Each one has its own job.

## Learn

**The left rail**, from top to bottom:

| Icon | What it does |
|---|---|
| BrainWaves icon | The app menu: new page, new page from a template, quick find, recent pages, light or dark theme, keyboard shortcuts, and this course |
| Sidebar | Shows or hides the sidebar (**Ctrl \\**) |
| Home | Your pages as a tree |
| Inbox | The page everything shared from your phone lands in |
| Tags | Every #tag, and the pages that use it |
| Starred | Your favourite pages |
| Templates | Your templates |
| PARA and Burrow | Two plugins (lessons 13 and 16) |
| Archived and Trashed | Pages you've put away, and pages you've deleted |
| Settings (at the bottom) | Theme, text size, page width, account, import, widgets, modules |

**The sidebar** shows what you picked in the rail. In **Home**, pages are a tree: a page can have
sub-pages, as deep as you like. Drag a page onto another to move it inside, or between two pages
to reorder. Hover a page for **+** (a new sub-page) and **⋯** (more).

**The middle** has your open pages as **tabs** at the top, with the **search** icon on the right.
Under the tabs, the **breadcrumbs** show where the page is, and on the right of that row:
document or canvas view, the **★** (star the page), copy as Markdown, and **⋯** (more: page
theme, sub-pages, duplicate, archive, move to trash and more).

**The right panel** shows the graph of linked pages, backlinks, unlinked mentions and a list of
keyboard shortcuts. **Ctrl Shift \\** shows or hides it.

**Finding anything:** **Ctrl K** opens quick find. Type part of a title or of any text; Enter opens
it.

**Putting pages away:** **Archive** (in a page's ⋯ menu) hides a page from the tree but keeps it
searchable and linked. **Move to trash** deletes it, and the Trashed view can bring it back.

## Try it

- [ ] Make a page called "Projects", then a sub-page under it with **+** next to it in the tree.
- [ ] Drag a page in the tree to reorder it.
- [ ] Star a page with **★**, then find it under **Starred**.
- [ ] Open two pages in tabs and switch with **Ctrl Tab**; close one with **Ctrl W**.
- [ ] Press **Ctrl K** and open a page by typing a word from it.
- [ ] Archive a page, find it under **Archived**, and bring it back.
- [ ] Hide and show the sidebar with **Ctrl \\**.
