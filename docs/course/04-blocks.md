# Blocks with more power

*Module 2: Writing well · about 15 minutes*

Beyond text and lists, BrainWaves has blocks that help you structure a page: make things stand
out, hide details until you need them, and lay things side by side.

## Learn

- **Callouts** (`/callout` or `!> `): a highlighted block with an icon. Click the icon for its
  look: ready-made styles (Note, Info, Tip, Warning, Danger, Quote), any colour, and Soft,
  Outline, Bar or Solid. **Save as a style** keeps a look to reuse.
- **Toggles** (`/toggle` or `>> `): the first line is a summary, everything under it folds away.
  Enter on an empty last line leaves the toggle.
- **Folding:** headings and list items with sub-items fold too. Click the arrow in the margin, or
  press **Ctrl Enter** in the block. A folded heading hides its whole section.
- **Columns** (`/2 columns`, `/3 columns`, `/4 columns`): put blocks side by side. Drag the line
  between columns to resize, **+** adds a column.
- **Tables** (`/table`): a simple table of text. **Tab** moves to the next cell (and adds a row
  at the end); a toolbar adds and removes rows and columns.
- **Images** (`/image`), **code blocks** (```` ``` ````), **quotes**, **dividers** (`---`).
- **Turn into:** click a block's **⠿** handle and choose **Turn into** to change its kind.

## Try it

- [ ] Add a callout with `!> `, then change its style to Warning.
- [ ] Give a callout your own colour and **Save as a style**.
- [ ] Make a toggle with `>> `, write a summary and two lines under it, then fold it.
- [ ] Fold a heading section with **Ctrl Enter**.
- [ ] Make two columns and put a list in each.
- [ ] Make a 3 × 3 table and fill it in with **Tab**.
- [ ] Turn a paragraph into a quote with the **⠿** handle.
