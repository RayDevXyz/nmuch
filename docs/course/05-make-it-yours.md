# Make it yours

*Module 2: Writing well · about 15 minutes*

A workspace you like looking at is one you come back to. BrainWaves lets you style the whole app,
and each page on its own.

## Learn

- **Icons:** hover a page's title and click **Add icon** for an emoji. It shows in the tree,
  tabs and links.
- **Headers:** **Add header** puts a gradient, colour or picture across the top, with the title
  on it, and optionally breadcrumbs and links to the sub-pages. **Edit header** has the details:
  height, title position, darkening, text colour.
- **The app theme:** Settings (bottom of the rail) has light and dark mode, a theme for each mode
  (Dark, Light, Midnight, Forest, Ember, High contrast and your own), page width and text size.
- **The theme editor:** Settings → **Theme editor…** to change colours and fonts; a readability
  check warns about text that's hard to read. You can import **VS Code themes** (a `.json` file
  or a whole `.vsix` extension).
- **A page's own theme:** hover the title and click **Theme**. Sub-pages follow their parent, so
  one theme can style a whole project.

## Try it

- [ ] Give three pages an icon.
- [ ] Add a header to your "Projects" page and show its sub-pages in it.
- [ ] Switch between light and dark mode in Settings.
- [ ] Open the theme editor and make your own copy of a theme with a different accent colour.
- [ ] Give "Projects" its own page theme and check that its sub-pages follow it.
