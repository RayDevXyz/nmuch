# Databases everywhere

*Module 3: Structure · about 10 minutes*

A database lives on one page, but you'll want to see it in many places: your tasks on the project
page, your reading list on your home page. And some lists of pages should simply keep themselves
up to date.

## Learn

- **Linked databases** (`/linked database`): show an existing database on another page. Pick
  **A new view just for this page** to filter and sort it there without changing the database's
  other views. Edits go to the same rows.
- **Maps of content** (`/map of content` or `/moc`): a list of links that keeps itself up to date.
  Its gear picks what it shows: this page's sub-pages, pages with a tag, rows of a database, or
  all pages; how deep, sorted and grouped how, as a list, columns or cards.

## Try it

- [ ] On your "Projects" page, add `/linked database` showing your Reading list, in a new view
  just for this page, filtered to Status = Reading.
- [ ] Tick off a row there and check it changed in the original database too.
- [ ] Add a map of content to "Projects" that lists its sub-pages, then add a new sub-page and
  watch it appear.
- [ ] Make a second map of content that shows every page tagged `#learning`, as cards.
