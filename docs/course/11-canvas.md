# The canvas

*Module 4: Visual thinking · about 20 minutes*

Some thinking doesn't fit in a straight line. Every page can also be a **canvas**: a large board
where its text sits in notes you can place anywhere, next to shapes, text, pictures, connectors
and frames. It's still the same page: the notes are its text in document view too.

## Learn

- **Switch:** the document and canvas buttons in the breadcrumb row, or **Ctrl Shift E**.
- **Move around:** scroll to pan (or hold **Space** and drag), **Ctrl** + scroll to zoom,
  **Ctrl 1** to fit everything.
- **Tools:** **N** note, **T** text, **R** rectangle, **O** ellipse, **D** diamond, **C**
  connector (drag from one thing to another), **F** frame, **V** select, **H** pan. Pictures come
  from the side panel on the left.
- **Styling:** the bar at the top: colours, borders, fonts, size, transparency, order.
- **Arranging:** smart guides line things up as you drag (hold **Ctrl** to move freely);
  **Ctrl G** groups, **Ctrl Shift G** ungroups, **Alt Shift L** locks.
- **Split a page into parts:** on a note, **✂** splits at the cursor and **≡** at each heading,
  so you can lay a long page out as separate cards.
- **Present:** frames become slides with **▶** in the zoom bar.

## Try it

- [ ] Open a page with a few headings and switch to the canvas with **Ctrl Shift E**.
- [ ] Split its note at each heading with **≡**, then move the parts around.
- [ ] Add three shapes, write in them, and connect them with **C**.
- [ ] Put a frame around two of them, and drag the frame by its title.
- [ ] Select a few items and group them with **Ctrl G**.
- [ ] Switch back to the document view and see your text there.
