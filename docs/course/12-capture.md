# Capture

*Module 5: A system for your life · about 10 minutes*

The first habit of a second brain is **capture**: the moment a thought, a link or a task comes
up, put it somewhere you trust, in a few seconds, without deciding yet where it belongs. Sorting
comes later (lesson 13).

## Learn

BrainWaves gives you several quick ways in:

- **The Inbox page:** the **Inbox** icon in the left rail opens it (it's made the first time).
  Anything shared from your phone lands at its top, with the date.
- **Sharing from your phone:** install BrainWaves on Android (lesson 16), then in any app tap
  **Share → BrainWaves**: **Add to Inbox**, or **New page** under it. It even works offline and
  catches up later.
- **The PARA capture box:** with PARA set up (lesson 13), type in the box at the top of the PARA
  panel and press **Enter**. The thought becomes a note in the PARA Inbox.
- **Buttons:** a "Quick note" button (lesson 10) on a starred page makes a new note in one click.
- **Quick find:** **Ctrl K**, type, and open or make what you need without touching the mouse.
- **Burrow:** if you use Burrow for bookmarks, links you save there can be dragged into any page
  (lesson 16).

## Try it

- [ ] Open the **Inbox** from the left rail and add three things on your mind right now.
- [ ] Make a "Quick note" button on a page and star that page.
- [ ] Capture something with the button, then find it again with **Ctrl K**.
- [ ] Decide on *one* place you'll always capture into first, and write it at the top of your
  Inbox.

> [!TIP]
> Capture should take seconds. If you find yourself deciding where something goes, stop: put it
> in the Inbox and move on.
