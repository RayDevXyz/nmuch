# PARA

*Module 5: A system for your life · about 20 minutes*

**PARA** (by Tiago Forte) sorts everything you keep into four places:

- **Projects:** things with a goal and a deadline ("Run a marathon by April").
- **Areas:** parts of your life you keep up, with no end date ("Health", "Home").
- **Resources:** topics you collect for later ("Running plans", "Design inspiration").
- **Archive:** anything from the other three that isn't active any more.

BrainWaves adds an **Inbox** in front of it: capture first, sort later.

## Learn

- **Set it up once:** **PARA** in the left rail → **Set up PARA**. You get a PARA page with four
  databases: Inbox, Projects, Areas and Resources, linked together, each with ready-made views and
  a page template.
- **The PARA panel:** a capture box, the four lists with counts, **To triage** (the Inbox), **Reviews
  due**, the health of your areas, your **Next actions**, and the whole **Archive**.
- **Triage** a note in the Inbox:
  - 🚀 **Promote to project**: give it an outcome, a next action, a deadline and an area. The note
    becomes the project, text and all.
  - ✓ **Link & process**: link it to the projects, areas and resources it belongs to.
  - 🗄 **Archive** it, with a reason.
- **Areas** have a standard, a health and a review cadence. An area whose review is overdue shows
  in **Reviews due**; **Mark reviewed** clears it.
- **Archive** anything with a reason (completed, canceled, on ice, deprecated). It leaves the lists
  but stays in links and the graph, muted, and you can restore it.

## Try it

- [ ] Set up PARA from the left rail.
- [ ] Capture three thoughts in the capture box.
- [ ] Add two areas, "Health" and "Home", with a monthly review cadence.
- [ ] Promote one thought to a project, with an outcome, a next action and the area "Health".
- [ ] Link another thought to "Home" with **Link & process**.
- [ ] Archive the third one as "On ice", then restore it from the Archive list.
- [ ] Open a project and check that its page started from the project template.
- [ ] Mark an area as reviewed.

> [!TIP]
> Do a weekly review: empty the Inbox, check each project has a next action, and clear the
> Reviews due list.
