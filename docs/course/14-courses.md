# Courses

*Module 6: Built-in modules · about 10 minutes*

The **Courses** module turns a folder of videos and documents into a course you can track: which
lessons you've watched, what's next and how far you are. It's ideal for online courses you've
downloaded. It needs the desktop app (it reads folders on your computer).

## Learn

- **Make a tracker (once):** type `/course tracker` in a page. You get two databases: **Courses**
  and **Lessons**.
- **Import a course:** on Courses, **Import a course folder**, then pick a folder. Every video and
  document becomes a lesson, in order, with subfolders as sections and video lengths read from the
  files.
- **The folder stays connected:** new files become lessons by themselves; removed files are marked
  missing (never deleted, so your ticks stay).
- **Watch and track:** press **▶** next to a lesson's file to open it (it notes *Last watched*),
  and tick **Done**. The course's **Progress** updates straight away.
- **See progress anywhere:** `/course dashboard` shows a card per course.
- **Privacy:** the module only reads folders you picked, and never changes your files. Settings →
  **Modules…** shows them.

## Try it

- [ ] Make a course tracker with `/course tracker` in a new page.
- [ ] Make a folder with a few files (videos or PDFs) in two subfolders, and import it.
- [ ] Open a lesson with **▶** and tick it **Done**.
- [ ] Look at the course's progress, and at the **To watch** view of Lessons.
- [ ] Add a `/course dashboard` to your home page.
