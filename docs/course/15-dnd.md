# Dungeons & Dragons

*Module 6: Built-in modules · about 20 minutes (skip it if you don't play)*

BrainWaves has a full toolkit for tabletop role-playing: a **compendium** of rules from your own
5etools, **campaigns** with story beats and world maps, **character sheets**, and a **table**
where your players join your battle maps from their own devices.

## Learn

- **Connect your 5etools (once):** **Ctrl Shift D** opens the compendium; its ⚙ takes your 5etools
  address. Nothing from 5etools is copied into your notes.
- **The compendium:** search spells, monsters, items, races and classes; follow links; put an
  entry in a page as a card with `/spell`, `/monster` or `/item`.
- **Campaigns:** `/campaign` makes one: NPCs, locations, factions, sessions, story beats (plan an
  arc from a template) and a world map where you pin pages (**I** for the map image, **P** for a
  pin).
- **Characters:** `/character` builds one from your 5etools races, classes and backgrounds, with a
  themed character sheet.
- **Battle maps and the table:** on a campaign, **+ Battle map** opens a canvas: the map image
  (**I**), tokens (**K**) and fog of war you reveal (**E**). **Go live** gives you a link for your
  players; they see the map live on their own devices. **Alt-click** pings a spot.

## Try it

- [ ] Connect your 5etools in the compendium's ⚙ (if you have one running).
- [ ] Search for "Fireball" and put it in a page as a card.
- [ ] Make a campaign with `/campaign` and add an NPC and a location.
- [ ] Plan a story arc from a template on the campaign page.
- [ ] Make a character with `/character`.
- [ ] Make a battle map, place a token and reveal part of the fog.
