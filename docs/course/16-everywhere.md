# Your server, laptop and phone

*Module 7: Everywhere · about 15 minutes to read (setting up a server takes longer)*

BrainWaves runs as a **desktop app**, as a **web app** on your own server, and on your **phone**.
Your notes are yours: they live on your computer or your server, nowhere else.

## Learn

- **Your own server:** run BrainWaves with Docker (or Cosmos Cloud) on a home server or VPS, with
  your own login. The first account made is yours.
- **API tokens:** Settings → **Account and API tokens** makes tokens for the desktop app, Burrow
  and your own scripts.
- **Two-way sync:** in the desktop app, Settings → **Sync with a server…**, enter your server's
  address and a token. Edits on either side meet in the middle, and it works offline: changes
  wait until you're back. A chip next to the search icon shows **Synced**, **Syncing** or **Not
  synced**.
- **Your phone:** open your server's address in Chrome and **⋮ → Install app**. You get a phone
  layout, a place in the share menu, and shortcuts (**New page**, **Inbox**) on the icon.
- **Burrow:** connect your Burrow bookmarks (the **Burrow** icon in the rail): search them, drag
  them into pages as cards, and save pages back to Burrow, with shared tags.

## Try it

- [ ] Read the hosting guide and decide where your BrainWaves will live.
- [ ] Make an API token in Settings → **Account and API tokens** (on a hosted BrainWaves).
- [ ] Connect the desktop app with **Sync with a server…** and check the sync chip.
- [ ] Install BrainWaves on your phone and share a link into the Inbox.
- [ ] If you use Burrow: connect it and drag a bookmark into a page.
