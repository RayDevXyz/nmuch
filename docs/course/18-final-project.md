# Final project: build your second brain

*About an hour, spread over a week*

You've seen everything BrainWaves offers. Now put it together into a workspace that works for
you. There's no right answer; here's a plan to start from.

## The plan

1. **Home base.** Make a page "Home" and star it. Give it a header and an icon.
2. **Capture.** Put a "Quick note" button at the top of Home, and set up PARA.
3. **Your lists.** Make a database for something you track (books, workouts, recipes), with a
   board view and a template for new pages.
4. **One dashboard.** On Home, add a linked view of that database filtered to what's current, a map
   of content of your PARA projects, and a formula that counts something you care about.
5. **Your look.** Pick a theme, or make your own in the theme editor.
6. **Every week.** Do a PARA review: empty the Inbox, give each project a next action, mark your
   areas reviewed.
7. **Everywhere.** Install BrainWaves on your phone and capture something on the go.

## Try it

- [ ] Home page with header, icon, star and a Quick note button
- [ ] PARA set up, with at least one project and two areas
- [ ] A database of your own, with a board view and a template for new pages
- [ ] Home shows a linked database, a map of content and a formula
- [ ] Your own theme
- [ ] One weekly review done
- [ ] Something captured from your phone
