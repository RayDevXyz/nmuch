# The BrainWaves course

A hands-on course that walks you through everything BrainWaves can do, from your first page to
a complete second brain. Each lesson explains one part of the app, then gives you a short
**Try it** list to do in your own workspace. Most lessons take 10 to 15 minutes.

> [!TIP]
> Take the course inside the app: open the app menu (the BrainWaves icon at the top of the left
> rail) and choose **Learn BrainWaves**. The lessons become pages in your workspace, and you can
> tick off each Try it step as you go.

## Module 1: The basics

- [1. Your first page](01-first-page.md): pages, blocks, the `/` menu and Markdown shortcuts
- [2. Finding your way around](02-finding-your-way.md): the left rail, the sidebar, tabs and search
- [3. Linking your thoughts](03-linking.md): links, backlinks, tags and the graph

## Module 2: Writing well

- [4. Blocks with more power](04-blocks.md): callouts, toggles, columns, tables and more
- [5. Make it yours](05-make-it-yours.md): icons, headers, themes and page themes
- [6. Properties and formulas](06-properties-formulas.md): facts about a page, and live values in your text

## Module 3: Structure

- [7. Databases](07-databases.md): tables, boards and galleries whose rows are pages
- [8. Databases everywhere](08-databases-everywhere.md): linked databases and maps of content
- [9. Templates](09-templates.md): pages that start ready-made, with fields to fill in
- [10. Buttons](10-buttons.md): one click to make a note, add a to-do or log a row

## Module 4: Visual thinking

- [11. The canvas](11-canvas.md): your page as a board of notes, shapes and connectors

## Module 5: A system for your life

- [12. Capture](12-capture.md): get everything out of your head and into one Inbox
- [13. PARA](13-para.md): Projects, Areas, Resources and an Archive

## Module 6: Built-in modules

- [14. Courses](14-courses.md): follow a course from a folder of videos
- [15. Dungeons & Dragons](15-dnd.md): the compendium, campaigns, characters and the table

## Module 7: Everywhere

- [16. Your server, laptop and phone](16-everywhere.md): hosting, sync, the phone app and Burrow
- [17. Keyboard mastery](17-keyboard.md): the shortcuts that make BrainWaves fast

## Final project

- [18. Final project: build your second brain](18-final-project.md): put it all together

When you've finished, the [guides](../README.md) go into every feature in more depth.
