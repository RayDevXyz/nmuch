Local-first · open source · yours

Your notes, databases and canvases, in one place that stays yours.

BrainWaves is a block-based workspace for writing, linking and organising. It runs on your computer or your own server, never in someone else's cloud.

Everything in one place

Write, link, organise, plan.

The things you'd otherwise spread across a notes app, a spreadsheet and a whiteboard, working together.

Block editor

Type / for anything: headings, to-dos, tables, columns, callouts, code and images. Markdown shortcuts work as you type.

Linked thinking

[[Page links]], ((block references)), backlinks, tags and a graph of how everything connects.

Databases

Typed columns, relations and rollups, with table, board and gallery views. Every row is a page.

Canvas

Open any page as a board of notes, shapes, connectors and pictures, with layers, guides and groups.

Templates & formulas

Pages from templates that ask for what changes. Live values in your text, such as [[Books]].count(Read).

Modules & widgets

Add whole workflows: follow a course, run a D&D campaign, or write your own widget in a single HTML file.

Databases

Structure when you need it.

A database is a page whose rows are pages too, so the data and the writing stay together.

  • Table, board and gallery views, each with its own filters and sorting
  • Relations between databases, and rollups that count and total across them
  • Turn any page into a database, or show one on another page
  • Maps of content that list pages by tag, folder or database and keep themselves up to date

Canvas

Think on an infinite board.

Every page can also be a canvas, and its text comes along as notes you can place anywhere.

  • Notes, shapes, text, connectors, frames, pictures and pins
  • A design-tool feel: a side panel, smart guides, layers, rotation and groups
  • Split a page into parts and lay them out; the document still reads in order
  • Present frame by frame, like slides

Make it yours

Your look, your workflows.

Shape the workspace around how you work.

  • A theme editor, and a different theme for any page
  • Page headers with pictures or gradients, breadcrumbs and sub-page links
  • Properties on any page, in a pop-up or under the title
  • Templates with fields to fill in, from a form or with {{template: …}}
  • Callouts in your own colours and styles

Modules

Run your D&D campaign in it.

The Dungeons & Dragons module turns BrainWaves into a game master's desk, and the Courses module tracks learning from a folder of lessons.

  • A searchable compendium, campaigns, story beats and character sheets
  • World maps with pins, including imports from Azgaar's generator
  • Live battle maps your players join from their own devices, with fog, tokens and initiative

Runs where you do

Desktop app, your server, your phone.

Keep everything in one file on your computer, host it on your own server, or both, kept in sync.

Desktop app

Windows, macOS and Linux. Your workspace is one SQLite file on your computer, and it works offline.

Self-hosted

One Docker image for Cosmos Cloud or Docker Compose, with your own login and API tokens.

Two-way sync

The desktop app and your server stay in sync. Write offline, and edits from both places merge.

On Android it installs as an app, with a place in the share menu.
